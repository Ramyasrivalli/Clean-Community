Simple hygiene, every day

A few small habits can protect your family and your whole community.

Wash hands with soap

Wash before eating, after using the toilet, and after cleaning.

Drink clean water

Boil, filter, or use safe water. Keep drinking water covered.

Use toilets properly

Always use a toilet and keep it clean for the next person.

Separate your waste

Keep wet waste and dry waste in separate bins at home.

Keep surroundings clean

Do not throw waste in streets, drains, ponds, or open land.

Stop mosquito breeding

Do not allow water to collect in pots, tyres, or containers.

Maintain personal hygiene

Bathe regularly, wear clean clothes, and keep nails short. Good hygiene keeps you confident and healthy.

My cleanliness promise

I will keep my home and surroundings clean, and encourage others to do the same.
