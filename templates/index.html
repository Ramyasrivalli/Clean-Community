Keeping our village clean is everyone’s responsibility.

Clean surroundings prevent illness, protect children, and make life better for every family.

Simple things that matter

Learn about a healthy community

Easy habits can keep our families, water, and surroundings safe.

Proper waste disposal

Put waste in bins and keep streets, drains, and common spaces clean.

Personal hygiene

Wash hands with soap before eating and after using the toilet.

Clean water

Keep drinking water covered and report any unsafe water source quickly.

Government awareness

Join local cleanliness drives and share healthy habits with neighbours.
